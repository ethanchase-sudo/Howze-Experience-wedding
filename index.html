Our Story

GROOM
Cameron Howze
BRIDE
Shambria Love

In a world where people swipe right… Cameron looked across the room.

The moment Cameron saw Shambria, he had a feeling she was someone special. There was just one small problem — they hadn’t officially met yet. Most people would’ve waited for fate. But Cameron? He came up with a master plan.

Chapter I

Operation: Meet My Future Wife

With the help of a friend, Operation: Meet My Future Wife was officially underway. Shambria was invited out for some food with friends, completely oblivious to the fact that she had just became the leading lady in Cameron’s master plan.

Chapter II

As if right on cue… Cameron appeared.

Coincidence? I think NOT! He casually offered to pay for her food, introduced himself, and what looked like an ordinary lunch became the opening to a great love story.

Spoiler alert: Cameron’s plan worked.
Chapter III

The best sequel yet… happily ever after

That first conversation turned into countless dates, endless laughs, unforgettable memories, and a love that continues to grow stronger with time.

Now, after years of choosing each other every single day, Cameron and Shambria are getting ready for the best sequel yet… happily ever after.

Special thanks to Ari for her supporting role in the making of this love story.

Questions & Answers

When is the RSVP deadline?

We kindly ask our guests to RSVP before January 6, 2027. You can respond right here on the website.

Where are the ceremony and reception?

Both take place at The Durham Armory, 220 Foster Street, Durham, NC 27701. The ceremony begins at 6:30 PM with the reception to follow.

Which airport should I fly into?

Raleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU) — the closest major airport, about 20 minutes from downtown Durham.

Where should I stay?

We recommend the Hampton Inn (1542 North Gregson Street, 919-688-8880) or the Hilton Garden Inn (2102 West Main Street, 919-286-0774). Please let us know if you are looking for accommodations.

Can I change my RSVP after submitting?

Yes. After you RSVP you’ll receive a personal confirmation code. Choose “Update my RSVP” and enter your email and code to make changes.

Is there a registry?

Your presence is all we ask for. If you’d still like to contribute, our registry is on Amazon.

Is there a wedding hashtag?

Yes — #TheLoveHowzeExperience. We’d love to see your photos!
